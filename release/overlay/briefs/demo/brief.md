# Northwind Transit: getting started with your travel card

*A fictional brief for trying out the system. Northwind Transit is not a real company.*

## Brief

- **Client:** Northwind Transit (city buses, trams and ferries)
- **Deliverable:** one 16:9 instructional explainer, about 70 seconds with the open, chapter cards and outro, for the website and station screens
- **Audience:** new riders, including visitors who have never used a tap-on card
- **Tone:** calm, friendly, practical. Plain words, no jargon.
- **Assets:** none. There are no app screens: carry the film with icons, numbers and simple diagrams.
- **Brand:** brands/demo.json (Avenir Next, mint green on white)
- **Must say:** the $10 minimum top-up, the 30% off-peak saving, register your card.

## Voiceover script

Spoken exactly as written. Each paragraph is one beat of the plan.

Getting around with Northwind is easy. Here's how to start riding with a travel card.

Step one: get your card. Pick up a card at any station, order one online, or add a digital card to your phone.

Step two: add value. Top up from as little as ten dollars. Turn on auto top-up and your card refills itself when it runs low.

Step three: tap on, tap off. Tap your card when you board, enjoy the ride, then tap off when you get off. We work out the lowest fare for you.

Step four: travel off-peak. Ride outside the morning and evening rush and you save thirty percent on every trip.

Step five: protect your card. Register your card online. If it's ever lost or stolen, we'll cancel it and move your balance to a new one.

You're all set. See you on board.
