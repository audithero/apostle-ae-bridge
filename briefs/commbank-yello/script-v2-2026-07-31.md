## Video 1: Introduction to Yello Changes

Purpose: Introduction into points and the program more broadly. Duration ~2mins for Frontline teams
and customers if we want to host within owned channels such as the change page. This will likely be
the first time the frontline have heard on these changes so want to give them the information to
fully understand the changes before commencing the e-learning and learning series.

> Amended Script — note supers have been removed, to be added after copy finessing.

### [SECTION – INDUSTRY & PRODUCT CONTEXT]

VO: We're introducing a number of changes across the CommBank Yello and credit card programs as we evolve how customers are recognised and rewarded for their banking relationship. While we'll cover each change in detail, they all support a simpler and more flexible rewards experience for customers.

VO: At the same time you might see changes also following the Reserve Bank of Australia's announcement of industry-wide interchange reforms, which will reshape Australian credit card marketing from 1 October 2026. As the industry adapts to these changes, CommBank is evolving the reward and loyalty propositions to continue delivering value to customers.

### [SECTION – SNIPPET ON YELLO CHANGE]

VO: The biggest change for Yello is that from 1 October, we'll be introducing CommBank Yello points. Unlocking a whole new world of points and perks for our customers.

### [SECTION – SNIPPET ON BANKING EARNING & NOD TO CREDIT CARD CHANGES]

VO: Eligible customers can earn CommBank Yello points based on their relationship with the bank. Whether they're taking out new products, renewing existing ones, making purchases on eligible cards, or reaching key banking milestones – customers will have more ways to be rewarded.

VO: The biggest change will be for customers who currently earn points through a CommBank Awards credit cards. Eligible customers will transition to earning CommBank Yello points. The number of points earned on eligible purchases will depend on the customer's CommBank Yello tier: CommBank Yello, Plus, Gold or Diamond.

VO: This means customers can now be recognised for more than just transactions, earning points across a broader range of banking activities and relationships.

### [SECTION – SNIPPET ON EARNING POINTS THROUGH PARTNER STORES]

VO: For the first time at CommBank, customers will also be able to earn Yello points when shopping with participating brands digital stores that are accessible in the app through the CommBank Yello hub.

### [SECTION – SNIPPET ON EARNING POINTS BY ACTIVATING OFFERS & REMOVAL OF CASHBACK]

VO: In addition to the new brand digital stores, from 1 October, customers will also be able to earn CommBank Yello points by activating offers in the CommBank app from an expanded network of partners.

VO: It's important to know, this is in addition to the points earned through eligible credit card products and that this feature will be replacing the current cash back offers.

### [SECTION – SNIPPET ON USING POINTS]

VO: Once customers have generated a points balance, they can redeem their CommBank Yello points their way across daily essentials, travel, shopping, gift cards and more, through the new redemption portal. Making sure we're giving customers choice in how they want to be rewarded.

VO: Customers can also transfer their points to partner loyalty programs they already use.

### [SECTION – SNIPPET ON PERKS]

VO: Plus, they'll enjoy exclusive offers, discounts, and presale access to some of the best shows, events, and experiences in town.

VO: To find out more, visit commbank.com.au/commbankyello or search CommBank Yello on One.CBA
