## V/O

As you may have heard we've introduced changes across CommBank Yello and some of
our credit card products as we evolve how customers are recognised and rewarded for
their banking relationship.

To recap these core changes,

- From 1 October, we'll be introducing CommBank Yello points, unlocking a whole
  new world of points and perks for our customers.
- Customers will have more ways to earn and use rewards across their everyday
  banking, travel, shopping and participating brand programs.
- Following the closure of the CommBank Awards program on 29 September,
  eligible credit card customers will transition to earning CommBank Yello points,
  from 1 October, based on their CommBank Yello tier.

In addition to this we are excited to announce more ways in which we are rewarding our
customers for their eligible banking and giving them choice in how they are rewarded.

For CommBank customers, finding the best place for travel has never been easier.
Travel Booking lets customers search and book flights, hotels and car rentals in the
CommBank app. It also gives customers access to CommBank exclusive travel offers
on flights, hotels and car hire, as well as 24/7 support.

So, from 1 October, customers can use and earn CommBank Yello points with Travel
Booking. This means they can earn 5x points per $1 spent on hotel and car rental
bookings, and 2x points for every $1 spent on flight bookings.

Also, with Dine in Deals, customers can discover and redeem exclusive offers at
participating restaurants across Australia. From 1 October eligible credit card
customers will receive a monthly $10 Dining Credit for Dine in Deals provided by
EatClub, while CommBank Yello customers can earn up to 5 CommBank Yello points
per $1 spent on eligible dining purchases, making every meal even more rewarding.

As part of this exciting change, the way customers earn points on their CommBank
credit card and broader everyday banking activity will change too.
From 1 October customers will have more ways to earn and use Yello points across
daily essentials, shopping and travel, plus access to a wide range of redemption
partners.

Depending on their tier and eligible credit card type, customers can earn up to 1.5 Yello
points on every $1 spent.

And regardless of their tier, they'll earn 2x the Yello points for every dollar spent on
international transactions.

These are some ways we are rewarding Australians with points and perks for the
banking they do every day.

To find out more, visit commbank.com.au/commbankyello or search CommBank Yello
on One.CBA
